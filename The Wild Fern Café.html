Our Story

A slow corner of the day, high above the noise

The Wild Fern began with a simple wish: a place where the morning could take its time. We roast and brew in small batches, bake before the doors open, and set every table where the view does the talking.

Whether you arrive for a quiet espresso, a long brunch or the last light over the valley, we want you to leave a little lighter than you came.

  • Small-batch coffee, brewed to order
  • Bread and pastry baked fresh each morning
  • An open terrace made for slow afternoons

Signature Experiences

Four reasons to stay a little longer

Everything here is made to be enjoyed slowly. Pick a table, order something good and let the afternoon stretch.

Specialty Coffee

Single-origin beans, brewed by hand. Ask what is pouring today and we will match a cup to your mood.

Artisanal Food

Seasonal plates built around local produce, with bread and pastry baked in-house every morning.

Peaceful Ambience

Warm wood, soft light and music that never competes with conversation. Bring a book or leave the phone.

Outdoor Seating

Tables on the open terrace face the valley. Arrive at golden hour and stay as the lanterns come on.
